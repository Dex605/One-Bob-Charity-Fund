Simple & Transparent

How your bob reaches a child

Every shilling donated goes directly to buying and delivering what children need most. No big offices. No waste. Just real impact.

💳
Step 01
You Donate Any Amount

Even one shilling counts. Every donation is recorded and goes into the fund immediately.

🛒
Step 02
We Buy What is Needed

Our team buys food, school supplies, and clothing from local suppliers — keeping money in the community.

🚐
Step 03
We Deliver Directly

We travel to schools and communities. No middlemen, no delays — straight to the children who need it.

📸
Step 04
We Show You the Impact

Every delivery is photographed and shared. You will see exactly which children your bob helped.

📦

Our Founder

The story behind One Bob

Lisa Stacy · Founder & CEO · One Bob Charity Fund

I grew up in Croatia, in a home where love was present but money was not. My mother ran a small shop — her hands and that little counter were the only things standing between our family and hunger. I finished secondary school, worked multiple jobs to pay my own college rent, and saved everything I could. When the moment I had worked toward was taken from me by someone I trusted, something broke inside me — and something else was born. I left, built a new life, and eventually made my way to Florida. I started One Bob Charity Fund because I know what it feels like to be the child that the world forgot. And I know what one small act of kindness can do.
